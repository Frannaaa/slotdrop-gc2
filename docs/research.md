# SlotDrop business desk research

Checked October 27, 2026. This research informs the CP0 choices. It is not a customer survey, provider interview, live seat audit or partnership agreement. Published prices and policies can change.

## Recommendation

Keep the recurring local activity MVP and optional bring-a-friend pricing. Start supplier discovery in Katipunan and UP Village, with Cubao as a nearby research lead rather than a claimed launch partner area. Prioritize providers that explicitly release spare seats in sessions already confirmed to run and accept bookings within the chosen discovery window.

Keep 72 hours as a product planning choice. Each session has its own provider-set cutoff, initialized from an activity default. Do not force a universal minimum booking lead time.

## Evidence and implications

### Local activity supply and companion pricing

[Laro Ceramics' official site](https://laro-ceramics.com/) lists a Katipunan, Quezon City branch, beginner pottery classes and group offers advertised as up to PHP 500 off per person. Its catalog lists mug-making at PHP 3,000 and beginner wheel-throwing at PHP 3,200. This supports researching beginner activities and optional companion offers locally. It does not establish spare seats, a two-person threshold, or willingness to pay SlotDrop commission.

The [mug-making product page](https://laro-ceramics.com/products/mug-making-class-manila) describes a three-hour class, materials and firing, with finished pieces taking approximately three months. It also states no refunds/cancellations and permits one reschedule. Implication: an activity listing must explain inclusions and delayed outputs; a booking cancellation is not automatically an unpaid seat the platform may resell. SlotDrop's 2+ threshold and saved per-seat rate remain its own simplified rules.

### Price realism and preparation costs

[Bumi & Ashe's official Cubao catalog](https://bumiandashe.com/collections/single-session) lists mug-making at PHP 2,650, reduced from PHP 3,200, and free-form pottery at PHP 3,200. These are posted products, not a market average or a guaranteed rate for any date. They show that several fictional seed prices are below these particular pottery examples. Keep seed prices labeled synthetic; never attribute them to these studios.

The [Cubao class page](https://bumiandashe.com/products/workshops-in-cubao-expo-qc) describes materials, instructor-led formats, prepaid reservations, and rescheduling tied to confirmed teachers and other booked students. Implication: restrict the initial offer to sessions already going ahead. Extra participants still consume materials, instruction and processing; provider proceeds cannot be called profit.

### Cutoffs vary

[Studio Matisse's published policy](https://studiomatisse.myshopify.com/pages/studio-policies) requires reservations at least two days in advance through its own website. This is a counterexample to assuming all pottery can be booked a few hours ahead. It is a San Juan policy reference, not a QC supplier claim. SlotDrop would need that provider's explicit participation and compatible distribution policy.

For a 48-hour session cutoff and a 72-hour discovery horizon, the bookable overlap is only between 48 and 72 hours before start. After the cutoff, exclude the session even if seats remain. The synthetic 180/120/60/30-minute activity defaults demonstrate variation; they are not researched studio policies. A provider can set another explicit session cutoff.

### Competitors and differentiation

[FunNow's official overview](https://www.events.myfunnow.com/whyfunnow-en) promotes near-term urban experience discovery and instant booking. SlotDrop therefore cannot claim last-minute discovery alone as a unique invention. Its proposed scope is a small local catalog, transparent per-booking totals and provider-controlled companion rates. That is positioning to test, not proven differentiation.

[ClassPass's partner FAQ](https://classpass.com/partners/faqs) describes excess-capacity distribution, negotiated floors and integrated inventory tools. Its seats can remain available through direct channels; SlotDrop's manual reserved-allocation model is different. Do not present an exclusive allocation as the ClassPass mechanism or assume ClassPass's outcomes transfer to SlotDrop.

ClassPass does not supply a general 10% commission benchmark here. Keep 10% as SlotDrop's simple course-model rate; the source supports attention to provider proceeds, not the exact number.

No source above establishes the number of unsold seats, booking conversion, customer age/income distribution or SlotDrop profitability. Those unknowns remain unknown after desk research.

## Inspectable scenario economics

These inputs are fictional session1, not market measurements: original PHP 2,000; standard offer PHP 1,600; buddy offer PHP 1,520; two seats.

- Standard two-person booking: PHP 3,200 total, PHP 320 modeled commission, PHP 2,880 provider proceeds.
- Buddy booking: PHP 3,040 total, PHP 304 modeled commission, PHP 2,736 provider proceeds.
- The buddy offer gives up PHP 160 booking value: PHP 16 platform fee and PHP 144 provider proceeds.
- One additional buddy attendee contributes PHP 1,368 provider proceeds before their materials/service cost. The booking is beneficial only relative to an explicit alternative; if both people would otherwise pay the standard offer, it reduces proceeds.
- At an illustrative 8% / 10% / 12% fee on the same two-person buddy order, modeled platform fees are PHP 243.20 / PHP 304 / PHP 364.80 and provider proceeds PHP 2,796.80 / PHP 2,736 / PHP 2,675.20. These are sensitivity cases, not estimated acceptable market rates.

Profit and incremental demand cannot be calculated from posted prices alone. No provider or customer was contacted for this desk research.
